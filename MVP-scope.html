Event Planning App: MVP Scope

Pitch line: "They help you find vendors. We help you pull off the event."

1. Overview

A marketplace plus planning tool for family functions (wedding, haldi, mehendi, engagement, birthday, naming ceremony, housewarming). Users can plan an event, discover independent vendors by seeing their work, book them, and coordinate everything in one shared plan.

2. Demo story

A family enters their event details, gets a recommended list of vendors, tasks and things to buy, browses vendors by their work, books an artist, and watches the shared family plan update live when the artist confirms.
